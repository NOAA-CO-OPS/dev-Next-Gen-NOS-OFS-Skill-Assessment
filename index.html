Mean Summary Statistics  ?Top-level metrics aggregated across the OFS and selected time period. CSI measures spatial accuracy, and is the percent of observed and modeled ice that overlaps; a score of 100 means perfect overlap.

System-Wide Time Series ?Evolution of mean ice concentration and skill metrics through time. CSI measures spatial accuracy, and is the percent of observed and modeled ice that overlaps; a score of 100 means perfect overlap.

Modeled vs. Observed Ice ?Compares model output directly against the satellite-based National Ice Center (NIC) analysis. Points above the line indicate over-icing; points below indicate under-icing.

NIC Ice Concentration ?Satellite-based ice concentration from the National Ice Center (NIC) over the selected period.

OFS Ice Concentration ?Modeled ice concentration from GLOFS over the selected period.

Long-term Climatology ?Historical mean ice concentration grid for this specific time period.

RMSE ?Root Mean Square Error spatial distribution. Lower values indicate better model performance.

CSI ?Critical Success Index spatial distribution. Values closer to 100 indicate higher accuracy.

Mean Error ?Model minus NIC analysis. Identifies areas where the model systematically over-predicts or under-predicts ice.

Daily Error Evolution ?Animated GIF showing the daily spatial evolution of the mean error.

Ice Concentration Time Series ?Compares OFS modeled ice against the NIC analysis, including historical climatology bounds. The Skill Score compares the cell-to-cell daily variances between the model and NIC and the climatology and NIC. A Skill Score > 0 indicates the model better matches the NIC than the climatology.

Ice Extent & CSI Overlap ?Tracks the total ice extent (% of basin that has at least 10% ice concentration) over time alongside critical success index (CSI) overlap metrics. CSI 'hits' is the proportion of model domain where modeled and NIC ice extent overlap. 'False alarms' is the proportion of the domain where the model predicted ice but it was absent in the NIC. 'Misses' is the proportion of the domain where the model failed to predict ice that was present in the NIC.

RMSE Distribution by Concentration ?Shows if the model is more accurate at predicting lower or higher ranges of ice concentration.
